# FPAC Study Planner

A PQ4R study plan for the AFP FPAC exam (February 26, 2027) that re-plans your dates as you go.

## Files

- `index.html` is the app
- `manifest.webmanifest` holds the app name, colors and icons
- `sw.js` lets the app open without internet
- `icons/` holds the bunny icons

## Put it online with GitHub Pages

1. On github.com, create a new **public** repository named `fpac-planner`.
2. Click **uploading an existing file** and drag in `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `README.md` and the whole `icons` folder. Click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings > Pages**. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
4. After a minute or two, the page shows your link: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/fpac-planner/`

## Install it on your phone

1. Open that link in Chrome on your phone.
2. Tap the three-dot menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen > Install**).
3. The bunny icon appears with your other apps.

## Your progress

- Progress is saved only in the installed app on that phone. Nothing is uploaded.
- To move progress in, open the Claude version, tap **Copy backup** in the Backup panel, then paste it into the app under **Restore from a backup**.
- Copy a backup now and then and keep it in a private note. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it erases your progress.

## Updating the app later

Upload the new `index.html` to the same repository. Your progress stays, because it lives on the phone, not in the file.
